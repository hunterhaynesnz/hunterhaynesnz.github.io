01 — ESSAY The case for paying attention On noticing the small things, and what they have to tell us. 2026 ↗

Attention is a kind of generosity. It asks us to stay with a moment long enough to see it clearly: the light shifting across a room, a familiar voice telling a story we’ve heard before, the thought that arrives when we finally stop trying to have one.

I’m trying to practice this more often. Not because every ordinary thing is secretly extraordinary, but because a life is mostly made of ordinary things. It seems worth being there for them.

02 — NOTE A list for a slower afternoon A few things that make an unremarkable day feel like enough. 2026 ↗

Open the window. Make something warm. Read the same paragraph twice because the first time you were thinking about tomorrow.

Go for a walk without turning it into a task. Come back by a different street. Let the afternoon be an afternoon.

03 — CREATIVE The house at the end of the road A small story about a place that remembers everyone who leaves. 2025 ↗

By the time the house came into view, the rain had stopped. It always did, just before the last bend, as if the weather knew better than to follow them all the way home.

The porch light was on. It had been on for years.

04 — ESSAY Learning to begin before you’re ready The blank page is less intimidating once you give it something to work with. 2025 ↗

I used to think the beginning came after the idea was ready. Once I had the right words, the right plan, the right amount of certainty, then I could start.

But a first attempt is not a promise that you already know what you’re doing. It’s how you find out. The beginning can be rough. That’s what makes it a beginning.
